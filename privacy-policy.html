Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 16, 2025  ·  Effective date: May 16, 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to FlowPost ("we", "our", or "us"). FlowPost is a social media content scheduling and management platform that allows users to schedule, publish, and manage video content across social media platforms including TikTok.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our service. By using FlowPost, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, and profile details you provide when registering.
  • TikTok Account Data: When you connect your TikTok account via OAuth 2.0, we collect your TikTok Open ID, display name, avatar URL, and access/refresh tokens necessary to publish content on your behalf.
  • Content Data: Videos, captions, hashtags, and scheduling settings you submit for publishing.
  • Usage Data: Log data including IP address, browser type, pages visited, and timestamps to improve our service.
  • Device Information: Operating system, browser version, and device type for compatibility purposes.

3. How We Use TikTok Data

FlowPost integrates with the TikTok Content Posting API. We only access TikTok data that is strictly necessary to provide our scheduling and publishing service.

Specifically, we use your TikTok data to:

  • Authenticate your identity via TikTok OAuth 2.0
  • Publish video content to your TikTok account at your scheduled time
  • Retrieve your creator information (privacy levels, posting permissions) before each post
  • Display your profile information within our dashboard
  • Refresh your access token automatically to maintain a continuous session

We do not sell, rent, or share your TikTok data with any third parties for advertising or marketing purposes. We do not use your TikTok data to train AI models.

4. Data Retention

We retain your data only as long as necessary to provide our service:

  • Access tokens: Stored securely and refreshed automatically. Deleted immediately upon account disconnection.
  • Scheduled content: Retained until the scheduled post is published or deleted by you.
  • Account data: Retained until you request account deletion.
  • Logs: Retained for a maximum of 90 days for debugging and security purposes.

You may request deletion of all your data at any time by contacting us at the email below.

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal information:

  • All data transmitted between your browser and our servers is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS.
  • TikTok access tokens and client secrets are stored encrypted and never exposed in frontend code.
  • We use environment variables and server-side handling for all API credentials.
  • Access to personal data is restricted to authorized personnel only.

6. Third-Party Services

FlowPost integrates with the following third-party services:

  • TikTok for Developers: For content publishing via the official TikTok Content Posting API. Subject to TikTok's Privacy Policy.
  • Vercel: For hosting and serverless API functions. Subject to Vercel's Privacy Policy.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third-party services.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of all personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal data and connected accounts.
  • Withdrawal of Consent: Disconnect your TikTok account at any time from within our dashboard or directly via TikTok's app settings.
  • Portability: Request your data in a portable format.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at: privacy@flowpost.app

8. Children's Privacy

FlowPost is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately so we can delete it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
